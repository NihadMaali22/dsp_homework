Homework Case 1

Section 4 Assignment Example

Convolution of a delayed unit step with a shifted unit impulse:

$$x[n] = 2u[n - 2], \quad h[n] = \delta[n - 1]$$

Theoretical Result: By the sifting property of the discrete-time impulse, convolving any sequence with a shifted impulse $\delta[n - n_0]$ produces an exact time shift of $n_0$ samples:

$$y[n] = x[n - 1] = 2u[n - 3]$$

DTFT Convolution Property:

$$\mathcal{F}\{y[n]\} = X(f) \cdot e^{-j 2\pi f (1)} \equiv X(f) \cdot H(f)$$
Homework Case 2

Rectangular Boxcar Pulses

Convolution of two rectangular pulses of lengths $N_1 = 5$ and $N_2 = 4$:

$$x[n] = 3\,\text{rect}_5[n - 2], \quad h[n] = 2\,\text{rect}_4[n + 1]$$

Theoretical Result: The convolution of two boxcar pulses produces a trapezoidal sequence of duration $(N_1 + N_2 - 1) = 8$ samples on interval $n \in [1, 8]$.

DTFT Convolution Property: The spectral product of two Dirichlet sinc kernels matches the DTFT of the trapezoid to machine precision ($< 10^{-13}$).

Homework Case 3

Cosine Signal Filtered by Moving Average

Filtering a discrete cosine by a 3-point moving average low-pass filter:

$$x[n] = 2\cos(2\pi \cdot 0.1\,n), \quad h[n] = \frac{1}{3}\text{rect}_3[n]$$

Theoretical Result: The 3-point moving average acts as a low-pass filter, attenuating the cosine amplitude by $|H(0.1)| \approx 0.709$ with linear phase delay.

Discrete Comb / Acoustic Echo Filter

Simulating acoustic reflection in discrete time:

$$h[n] = \delta[n] + 0.6\,\delta[n - 4]$$

Theoretical Result: Produces a periodic comb filter magnitude response with constructive and destructive interference notches.
